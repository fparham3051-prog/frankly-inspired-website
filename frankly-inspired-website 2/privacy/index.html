Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 29, 2026

Frankly Inspired and Associates, LLC respects your privacy and is committed to handling the information you share with us responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect through this website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, submit an inquiry, schedule a conversation, or otherwise communicate with Frankly Inspired and Associates, LLC.

This information may include your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Organization
  • Professional title or role
  • Organization website
  • Areas of interest
  • Information you voluntarily provide regarding your organization's needs or challenges

We encourage visitors not to submit confidential, sensitive, financial, health, student, employee, donor, or legally privileged information through the general website inquiry form.

Free Tools and Your Health Index

Answers to the free assessment tools, and the combined Health Index built from them, are stored in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers. To generate a written summary and show anonymous comparisons, the tools send only your scores, without your name, email, or organization. If you use the optional organization lookup, the name or EIN you type is sent to our server and on to ProPublica's public Nonprofit Explorer to find your public Form 990 filings; we don't store your searches. Your email, scores, and the organization you selected are sent to us only if you choose to email yourself your results. You can delete your saved answers at any time from the Health Index page. If you ask the optional results assistant on the Health Index page a question, your question and your scores (never your name, email, or organization) are sent to our server and to Anthropic's AI service to generate the answer; we don't store them. If you ask for a 90-day re-check reminder, we store your email address and the date until the single reminder is sent, then delete it; you can cancel it at any time with the link we show you. See How scoring works for details.

Information Related to Paid Diagnostic Engagements

If you engage Frankly Inspired and Associates, LLC for a Formation Readiness, Grant Readiness, or Institutional Durability Diagnostic, the information involved is broader than a general website inquiry, and is handled differently.

For the Grant Readiness Diagnostic specifically, the Financial Health dimension is scored using your organization's IRS Form 990 filings, retrieved from ProPublica's public Nonprofit Explorer database and the IRS's public e-file data. These are publicly available records, not documents you need to submit to us.

For all three diagnostics, you may also choose to share additional organizational materials directly with us to support scoring and evidence review, for example governance policies, board materials, financial statements, or strategic plans. This is shared at your discretion, outside the general website inquiry form, typically by email or a shared file link agreed on during the engagement.

Information you share for a diagnostic is used only to complete that diagnostic: scoring, the written findings memo, and the readout session. It is not sold, and it is not reused for an unrelated engagement without your knowledge.

We retain diagnostic engagement records, including scoring inputs, the findings memo, and related correspondence, for seven years after the engagement concludes, consistent with standard recordkeeping practice for professional services and financial-related engagements. Within that window, you may request that we delete source materials you shared, such as financial statements, board materials, or governance documents, once the engagement concludes; we will retain only what we reasonably need to keep for that period.

  • Schedule meetings
  • Communicate regarding services
  • Improve our website and visitor experience
  • Maintain appropriate business records

Frankly Inspired and Associates, LLC does not sell personal information submitted through this website.

Third Party Services

The website and its diagnostic tools use third party providers to support hosting, forms, scheduling, email, data storage, analytics, and AI-assisted features. These currently include:

  • Render: website and application hosting
  • Cloudflare: hosting for the InstitutionalOS scorecard tools
  • Resend: transactional email delivery
  • Neon: database storage for anonymized assessment benchmark data
  • Anthropic (Claude): generates the personalized narrative summary on self-assessment tools; this only ever receives your numeric scores, never your name or organization
  • ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer: public IRS Form 990 data for the optional organization lookup
  • Calendly: scheduling
  • Google Workspace: business email and document handling

Information processed through these services may also be subject to the privacy practices of those providers.

Website Analytics and Cookies

This website uses essential cookies and similar technologies required for the website to function correctly, such as remembering your cookie preference.

To know which pages are useful, the website also counts page visits and a few actions (starting a free tool, opening the scheduler). These counts use no cookies and store only the page, the action, and the date. They do not record your IP address or anything else that identifies you.

This website may also use optional analytics cookies to understand general website traffic, visitor behavior, and website performance. Optional analytics cookies are only set if you accept them through the cookie banner shown when you first visit the site. If you decline, or take no action, no analytics cookies are set.

You can change your cookie preference at any time using the "Manage Cookie Preferences" link in the footer of any page.
