How the Diagnostic runs

  1. Before
    A free 30-minute call. The decision in front of you, by when, and who decides. If a Diagnostic isn't the right step, I'll say so. If it is, you get a one-page proposal within a few days.
  2. Week 1
    Kickoff. An intake with the executive director (and board chair if possible). I collect your 990s, budget, policies and org chart, and send a six-minute survey to your leaders and board.
  3. Weeks 2 to 3
    Scoring. Each area is scored on what the documents show, with a note on every score. Where what leaders say differs from the documents, I flag it.
  4. Week 4
    Readout. A 60 to 90 minute session: the scores, the memo naming the gap that will break first, and the 90-day plan. Your workspace opens the same day.
  5. Days 30 to 90
    The plan runs. Your team logs a 15-minute weekly check-in. At day 90 we review what moved and decide what's next. A six-month re-score is booked before we close.

Why a flat fee

The fee is set by the decision it protects, not the hours. Opening a second site too early, losing a multi-year grant, or hiring to fix a decision problem each costs far more than $2,500. A fixed fee and scope is also easy for a board or funder to approve.

Common questions

What documents will I need to share?

Your latest Form 990, this year's budget, bylaws, board minutes, and any strategic plan. For the Fund path I read your 990s from public records, so you don't need to send them.

Is this legal or accounting advice?

No. When something needs a CPA, an attorney or an evaluator, I'll refer you to one.

We're not ready to pay for a diagnostic. Where should we start?

Take the free check-up. It takes about eight minutes and shows where you are and the gap most likely to break first.

How do I make the case to my board?

Print your check-up results (there's a one-page board brief on the results screen) and bring them to the board. Your board chair is welcome at the readout. You can also ask a funder to cover it.

How is the check-up scored?

Each answer is scored from "not in place" to "verified." Every question and its source is on How Scoring Works.

Can a funder pay for the Diagnostic?

Yes. Use the letter above, and I can invoice the funder directly.
