How Frankly Inspired works

InstitutionalOS

The system behind every Frankly Inspired engagement. It scores your organization on seven areas, checks the evidence behind each score, names the one gap that will break first, and tracks a 90-day plan to close it, in a private workspace your team uses every week.

See inside an InstitutionalOS workspace

Every client gets a private workspace that stays with them across the whole engagement: one record of their scores, the evidence behind them, the plan, and every check-in. Shown here with a sample organization.

  1. 1 · Assess

    One Health IndexOne score from 0 to 100 across the seven areas, weighted for your stage (Build, Fund, or Sustain)., seven areas

    The organization is scored 0 to 100 across direction, proof of results, revenue, leadership, operations, partnerships, and governance. The weakest area, the one most likely to break first, is flagged in red.

  2. 2 · Verify

    Every score shows what backs it

    Each area carries the evidence behind its score: verified when a source document is attached, noted when it isn't, and flagged when nothing is on file yet. Scores without evidence are treated as assumptions.

  3. 3 · Decide

    The one area to fix first

    Each area gets a 90-day target. For your stage, a few areas have minimums that funders and boards expect, so you know what to fix first, even when the average looks fine.

  4. 4 · Act

    A 90-day plan the team works in every week

    Actions with owners, dates, and what counts as done. The team updates status and files a 15-minute weekly check-in in the same workspace, so blocked items surface right away.

  5. 5 · Review and reassess

    Progress you can see over time

    Every reassessment adds a point to the organization's history, so the board sees whether the work is moving the score, not just whether tasks got done.

  6. 6 · Before and after, ready for the board

    What changed, on one page

    Every area's score at the Diagnostic sits next to today's, so the change is plain to see. A two-page board report pulls it together: where you stand, what moved, the plan's progress, and the decisions ahead. See a sample board report (PDF).

Scores you have to earn

Evidence, not confidence

Each area is scored 0 to 100 on what you can show: an idea scores 25, something informal 50, documented and in use 75, routine and checked 100. A score with nothing behind it is treated as an assumption.

No hiding behind an average

Each stage has a few areas that must clear a minimum on their own. A strong overall score can't make up for weak governance before a launch, or thin proof of results before a major grant.

A target you can reach

The 90-day target closes a quarter of the gap: an area at 44 aims for 58. Financial health comes straight from your filed Form 990s, not estimates. Every score is rechecked at six months.
