The Method Behind Frankly Inspired

InstitutionalOS

Frankly Inspired helps leaders build institutions that are clear enough to launch, strong enough to fund, and disciplined enough to endure. InstitutionalOS is the operating system underneath that work: one shared way to diagnose institutional health, verify the evidence behind a score, clear the gates that actually matter, and track whether things get better over time, not another dashboard promising a shortcut.

The method: assess, verify, decide, improve, reassess

What makes this an operating system rather than a one-time questionnaire is that step five feeds back into step one. A score is a snapshot; the point is what changes between snapshots.

  1. Assess: complete the structured Health Index for your stage
  2. Verify: attach or confirm evidence for every score that claims more than "informal"
  3. Decide: clear the hard gates for your stage, or don't proceed yet
  4. Improve: close roughly a quarter of the gap on your single biggest constraint
  5. Reassess: score again and see what actually moved

The Health Index Engine

Seven dimensions, scored the same way at every stage. What changes stage to stage is which dimensions carry the most weight and which ones gate a "go" decision outright, regardless of the average.

Seven common dimensions

  1. Strategy and Positioning
  2. Program and Impact Evidence
  3. Revenue and Financial Health
  4. Leadership and Organizational Capacity
  5. Operations and Infrastructure
  6. Partnerships and Ecosystem
  7. Governance and Risk

Scoring rubric

Higher scores require evidence, not confidence. A score is set by what's documented and demonstrated, not by how the answer is worded.

What each score means
ScoreMeaning
0Absent: not in place

50Informal or incomplete: exists, but not documented or consistent
75Documented and demonstrated: real evidence on file
100Verified, repeatable, and institutionally embedded

Decision gates

An overall average never produces a green light by itself. Each stage defines a small set of dimensions that must individually clear a floor (governance, strategy, financial health, or program evidence, depending on the stage), so a weak spot can't hide behind a strong average.

Even inside a "go" band, a single open gate (ungoverned risk, missing filings, an unresolved dependency) can hold back a move-forward decision. That check happens in the paid InstitutionalOS Diagnostic, not the self-service scorecard.

The 90-day target: closing a quarter of the gap

The "Improve" step follows one fixed rule, not a guess: Target = Current Score + 25% × (100 − Current Score). A dimension scoring 44 gets a 90-day target of 58, a nearer, evidence-reachable goal for whichever dimension is the actual binding constraint, not an aspirational push toward 100 across the board. See it applied to a real (illustrative) scorecard on the Consulting pricing page.

Claims cannot outrun evidence

A score with nothing behind it isn't treated the same as a well-documented one, even when the number looks identical. Every dimension you score yourself carries a place for the evidence that justifies it (a note, a link, a filed document), and financial health is scored automatically from your actual ingested filing data rather than a self-reported estimate.

That evidence, and every score you've saved, lives in the Console's Evidence Vault: one place to see what backs each dimension, what's still unsupported, and how the score has moved since the last time you checked. Reassessment turns a one-time diagnostic into institutional memory instead of a number you get once and forget.
