How a cohort runs

  1. Select 5 to 10 grantees together, ideally at a decision point: expansion, a larger grant, a leadership change, or visible strain.
  2. Kickoff session for the cohort: what the Diagnostic measures and how results stay confidential.
  3. Individual Diagnostics, about four weeks each, run in parallel: intake, evidence review, readout, memo, and 90-day plan.
  4. Portfolio report and learning session once every grantee has had its readout.
  5. Six-month reassessment on the same rubric, so you see what moved.

The method

InstitutionalOS scores seven dimensions on one evidence rubric (not in place, talked about, informal, documented, verified): Strategy and Positioning; Program and Impact Evidence; Revenue and Financial Health; Leadership and Organizational Capacity; Operations and Infrastructure; Partnerships and Ecosystem; Governance and Risk. Higher scores require evidence a funder or board could check. Stage gates mean a strong average can't hide a critical weak spot. The full method is public at franklyinspired.associates/methodology.

What it is not

It isn't an audit, an evaluation of program impact, or grant writing. Where a grantee's risk is technical (financial controls, legal, formal evaluation), we flag it and refer to a specialist.
