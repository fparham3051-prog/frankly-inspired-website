The problem behind the problem

Nonprofits rarely struggle because the mission lacks value. They become fragile when the institution doesn't mature as fast as the programs, the funding, and the expectations.

36%of nonprofits ended 2024 with an operating deficit, the highest in ten years
52%hold three months of cash or less
57%of nonprofit CEOs say foundation grants have become harder to win since January 2025

Sources: Nonprofit Finance Fund, 2025; Center for Effective Philanthropy, 2026.

What it often turns out to be

Thin evidence behind the story. Outcomes are measured for one program, or none, so larger funders can't say yes.
