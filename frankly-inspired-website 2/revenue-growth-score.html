Revenue & Growth Score

Seven numbers from your most recent budget or financial statements, scored against the same reserve, spending-mix, revenue-diversification, and liquidity benchmarks that shape a strong fundraising and revenue-pipeline strategy. Takes about 10 minutes. Nothing you enter here leaves your browser.

Have your most recent annual budget, financial statements, or Form 990 on hand. Every figure below is a single line item you can pull directly from one of those — nothing needs to be calculated first.

Reserves

Cash and investments your board could actually use if needed — not funds restricted by a donor or grant to a specific purpose. Reserve depth is what buys time to grow revenue deliberately instead of chasing whatever funding shows up next.

By Metric

This is a directional estimate based on the figures you entered, using standard, publicly documented nonprofit financial ratios and commonly cited sector benchmarks. It is not a substitute for a review by a CPA or financial advisor, and it does not account for context specific to your organization (grant cycles, capital campaigns, planned deficits, and similar).

This tool measures revenue and growth capacity from your own budget.
