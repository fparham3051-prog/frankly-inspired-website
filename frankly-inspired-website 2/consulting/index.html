How We Work Together

Every engagement starts the same way: a discovery conversation, followed by the InstitutionalOS Diagnostic scored for your organization's actual lifecycle stage. From there, the method never changes, whether the priority is formation, funding, or long term durability.

  1. Assess. Structured scoring across the dimensions that matter at your stage, never a generic checklist, plus a six-minute survey of your executive director, board members, and senior staff, so we see where your leaders agree and where they don't.
  2. Verify. Higher scores require evidence: a documented policy, an observed practice, a filed financial statement, not self reported optimism. A strong score means something because it had to be earned.
  3. Decide. We name the binding constraint: the highest-leverage gap right now, the one that will stall your next funding, growth, or leadership decision if it is left alone. Naming it specifically is the job, not producing a wall of generic recommendations.
  4. Act. A 90-Day Improvement Plan for the binding constraint: one clear target, and three to six actions, each with an owner, a date, and the evidence that will show it's done. Concrete enough to act on Monday morning.
  5. Review. A 15-minute weekly check-in your team logs in its private client portal (what got done, what's blocked, what decision is needed), a monthly look at whether the diagnosis still holds, and a 90-day review.
  6. Make it standard. Before we close, we confirm the change is written down, owned, used consistently, taught to new staff, and measured, so it outlasts the engagement.
  7. Reassess. A full, evidence-based reassessment at six months shows what actually moved and which constraint is next.

This is the same method behind every free scorecard on this site. A paid diagnostic goes further: it verifies the evidence directly with you and ends in a working session, not just a number.

How we choose the constraint

When two gaps compete, we apply the same rule every time, in this order:

  1. A failed stage gate outranks a low average.
  2. The gap others depend on comes first. Weak evidence usually comes before weak fundraising.
  3. The gap tied to the decision in front of you comes first.

The runner-up is named in your memo as "next in line," so nothing is hidden.

When we bring in a specialist

We diagnose and help fix strategy, partnerships, revenue strategy, leadership capacity, and operations. Where the risk is technical, we flag it and refer you:

  • Financial controls and audits: a nonprofit CPA
  • Legal structure, employment, and compliance: an attorney
  • Formal impact studies: a program evaluator

We will also tell you when not to proceed yet, for example with an expansion or a major application.

What we don't do

  • Grant writing or proposal production
  • Bookkeeping, accounting, or audits
  • Legal formation and compliance filings
  • Open-ended staffing with no defined outcome

Saying so up front keeps every engagement focused on the one problem we are best at: finding what is holding the institution back.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does working together start?

With a free 30-minute conversation, then the InstitutionalOS Diagnostic: $2,000 for Formation, $2,500 for Grant Readiness or Institutional Durability. It's the single entry point for every paid engagement.

What do I get from the diagnostic?

All seven Health Index dimensions scored against your stage's benchmarks, a written memo naming your single binding constraint, a 90-Day Improvement Plan with owners and dates, a live readout session, and a private client portal where your team logs weekly check-ins. The 90-day review and six-month reassessment are scheduled before we close. See a sample.

What documents will I need to share?

For Grant Readiness, financial health is scored from your public IRS Form 990 filings, so you don't need to send them. For any diagnostic, you can choose to share governance policies, board materials, financial statements, or strategic plans to support the evidence review.

How is my organization's information handled?

What you share is used only to complete your diagnostic. It's never sold or reused for another engagement without your knowledge, and you can ask for shared source materials to be deleted once the engagement ends. See the Privacy Policy.

Is there a minimum commitment?

The advisory retainer has a three-month recommended minimum. Executive coaching is $350 per 60-minute session, scheduled as needed with no minimum.

Is this legal or accounting advice?

No. Where a finding touches legal, accounting, or safeguarding matters, you'll get a recommendation to bring in the right specialist rather than a substitute opinion.

We're not ready to pay for a diagnostic. Where should we start?

Take one of the free tools. The 7-minute self-assessment covers all seven dimensions and points you to your likely constraint. Every tool you take after that adds to your Health Index, one profile you can print for your board.

How do I make the case to my board?

Print your free Health Index and bring it to your next board meeting. It shows all seven dimensions on one page, along with your likely constraint. Your board chair is welcome at the diagnostic's readout session. Some funders also offer capacity-building grants that can cover outside consulting, so ask your program officers.

How are the free tools scored?

Every tool uses the same seven dimensions and the same evidence rubric, from "not in place" to "verified." Each question and its research source is published on How Scoring Works.

Can a funder pay for the Diagnostic?

Yes. Many funders support capacity building, and the fixed price makes it an easy request. Use the sponsorship letter, and the invoice can go directly to the funder.
