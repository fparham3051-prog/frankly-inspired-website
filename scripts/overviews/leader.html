The InstitutionalOS Diagnostic

Find the gap that will break first, and fix it in 90 days.

Growing nonprofits add programs, sites, and funding faster than they build the leadership, revenue, evidence, and systems to carry them. The Diagnostic finds which gap will stall your next funding, growth, or leadership decision, verifies it with evidence, and gives you a focused 90-day plan.

Five situations that call for it · one method and one price for each

Expansion ReadinessAdding a site, city, or major program. Can the organization carry it without weakening what works?
Funder ReadinessGoing after bigger funding, or a major grant is ending. Will your evidence, governance, and finances hold up?
Founder DependenceToo much depends on one leader. What has to be written down and handed off first?
Capacity StrainGrowing, but staff and systems are stretched. Is it headcount, or decision rights, workflow, or revenue quality?
Leadership TransitionA leadership change is coming. Which knowledge, relationships, and authority must transfer first?

How it works (about four weeks)

  1. Intake. A 60-minute call: what's happening and what you believe the problem is, in your words.
  2. Evidence review. Your 990, budget, board minutes, policies, and outcome reports, scored on all seven dimensions.
  3. Constraint call. We name the binding constraint and the one next in line, and test them against your stage's gates.
  4. Readout. 90 minutes with your executive director and board chair.
  5. Memo and 90-day plan. Each action has an owner and a date, plus what not to work on yet.
  6. Reassessment booked before we close, tracked in your private client portal.

What you receive

  • Verified scores on all seven dimensions, with the evidence behind each
  • A written memo naming the binding constraint and the next in line
  • A 90-day plan, including the steps to your next Candid Seal of Transparency level
  • A private client portal: scores, targets, progress, documents, and your next session
How we choose the constraint. A failed stage gate outranks a low average. The gap others depend on comes first. The gap tied to your decision comes first. The runner-up is named, so nothing is hidden.

When we bring in a specialist

We diagnose and help fix strategy, partnerships, revenue strategy, leadership capacity, and operations. Financial controls and audits go to a nonprofit CPA, legal and compliance questions to an attorney, and formal impact studies to an evaluator. We'll also tell you when not to proceed yet.
