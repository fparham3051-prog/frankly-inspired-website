Revenue & Growth Score

Nine numbers from your most recent budget, financial statements, and donor database, scored against the same reserve, spending-mix, revenue-diversification, liquidity, and donor-retention benchmarks that shape a strong fundraising and revenue-pipeline strategy. Takes about 10 minutes. Nothing you enter here leaves your browser. This is the deeper, numbers-based follow-up to the Revenue Diversification pillar in the free Self-Assessment: jump in below, or start there first for the faster 7-minute version.

Have your most recent annual budget, financial statements or Form 990, and a donor count from your CRM or donor database on hand. Every figure below is a single line item or count you can pull directly from one of those, nothing needs to be calculated first.

Reserves

Cash and investments your board could actually use if needed, not funds restricted by a donor or grant to a specific purpose. Reserve depth is what buys time to grow revenue deliberately instead of chasing whatever funding shows up next.

$

Spending Mix

Everything spent to run the organization for the year, programs, administration, and fundraising combined.

$

The portion of total expenses spent directly on programs and services, not administration or fundraising.

Revenue Diversification

All revenue for the year, from every source combined: the baseline for measuring how well your fundraising strategy and revenue pipeline are diversifying growth.

$

The largest grant, contract, or donor, whichever one, if lost, would hurt the most. This is the core number behind any donor stewardship, retention, or revenue-diversification strategy. If two funders are close, use the larger.

$

Short-Term Liquidity

Cash, receivables, and anything else convertible to cash within about 12 months. Excludes buildings, equipment, and other fixed assets.

$

Bills, payroll, and other obligations due within about 12 months.

Donor Retention

Every individual, household, or organization that made at least one gift in your prior fiscal year; pull this as a donor count from your CRM or donor database.

The count of last year's donors who made at least one gift again this year: the core number behind the Fundraising Effectiveness Project's donor retention rate, the standard national benchmark for this metric.

This is a directional estimate based on the figures you entered, using standard, publicly documented nonprofit financial ratios and commonly cited sector benchmarks.
